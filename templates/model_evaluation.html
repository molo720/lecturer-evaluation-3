Aspect extraction method and coverage

Aspect extraction currently uses a rule-based lexicon of aspect-specific keywords and curated phrase variants. It is not a learned sequence-labeling model, and unfamiliar wording may still be missed.

When no aspect phrase is matched, the analysis reports Insufficient evidence rather than treating the missing aspect as neutral. Synonym coverage is a practical improvement, but it does not guarantee semantic understanding; validate it against manually labelled comments before claiming a measured accuracy gain.
